I am a first-year graduate student pursuing my Master of Engineering in Mechanics at the **Department of Modern Mechanics**, [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/), supervised by [Assoc. Prof. Fenghua Qin](https://en.mecheng.ustc.edu.cn/). My research is centered on the **propulsion hydrodynamics of biologically inspired tandem undulating fins**, utilizing high-resolution Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and theoretical modeling to uncover wake vortex interactions and energy-harvesting mechanisms.

Prior to USTC, I received my B.Eng. in Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering from [Harbin Engineering University (HEU)](https://english.heu.edu.cn/) in 2024, graduating as an **Outstanding Graduate (优秀毕业生)** with a GPA of **89.1 / 100** (Rank **13 / 191**, Top 7%).

## Research Interests

- **Bionic Underwater Propulsion:** Undulating fin locomotion, ribbon-fin bio-robotics, wake vortex dynamics, and hydrodynamic energy harvesting.
- **Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD):** Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM, Palabos), Immersed Boundary Method (IBM), Large Eddy Simulation (LES).
- **High-Performance Computing (HPC):** Adaptive Mesh Refinement (AMReX), MPI/GPU hybrid acceleration, large-scale direct numerical simulations.
- **Marine & Offshore Hydrodynamics:** High-speed ship seakeeping, free-surface slamming, and non-linear wave loads (STAR-CCM+, ANSYS Fluent).
- **Quantum CFD (Frontier Exploration):** Quantum algorithms and quantum linear solvers applied to the discrete Boltzmann equation.
