I am a first-year graduate student pursuing my Master of Engineering in Mechanics at the **Department of Modern Mechanics**, [University of Science and Technology of China (USTC)](https://en.ustc.edu.cn/), advised by [Assoc. Prof. Fenghua Qin](https://en.mecheng.ustc.edu.cn/). My research focuses on the **propulsion hydrodynamics of biologically inspired tandem undulating fins**, utilizing high-resolution Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) and theoretical modeling to uncover wake vortex interactions and energy-harvesting mechanisms.

Prior to USTC, I received my B.Eng. in Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering from [Harbin Engineering University (HEU)](https://english.heu.edu.cn/) in 2024, graduating as an **Outstanding Graduate (优秀毕业生)** with a GPA of **89.1 / 100** (Rank **13 / 191**, Top 7%).

## Research Pillars & Focus

<div class="pillars-grid">

  <div class="pillar-card">
    <div class="pillar-icon"><i class="fas fa-fish"></i></div>
    <h3>Bionic Fin Propulsion & FSI</h3>
    <p>
      Hydrodynamic coupling, wake vortex interference, and energy harvesting in tandem undulating ribbon fins using the Lattice Boltzmann Method (Palabos), IBM, and LES. Formulated the analytical <b>Equivalent Phase Model</b> ($R^2 > 0.97$).
    </p>
