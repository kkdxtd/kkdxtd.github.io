This research investigates the seakeeping and severe green water slamming of a high-speed trimaran operating in regular waves (5th-order Stokes) and irregular waves (JONSWAP spectrum) using STAR-CCM+.

**Key Highlights:**
- Free surface captured via Volume of Fluid (VOF) method
- Six-degree-of-freedom ship motions solved using overset meshes and Dynamic Fluid–Body Interaction (DFBI)
- 1:32 scale model corresponding to a 140 m full-scale vessel; verified against towing tank model test measurements.
