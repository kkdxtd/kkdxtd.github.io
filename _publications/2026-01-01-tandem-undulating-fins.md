This study systematically investigates the hydrodynamic coupling and wake interference mechanisms of **tandem undulating fins** under a pure undulation mode, inspired by the high maneuverability and stealth of gymnotiform and balistiform fish locomotion.

**Key Contributions & Findings:**
- **Numerical Methodology:** Carried out high-fidelity simulations using the open-source Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM) software library **Palabos**, integrating Large Eddy Simulation (LES) with a regularized BGK collision operator and an Immersed Boundary Method (IBM) based on Inamuro's iterative velocity correction algorithm.
- **Cross-Dimensional Modeling:** Validated the physical rationality and computational efficiency of 2D cross-sectional simplifications against full 3D models for parametric screening.
- **Equivalent Phase Model:** Discovered periodic sinusoidal thrust variations of the posterior fin and innovatively formulated a semi-empirical analytical formula based on the **"Equivalent Phase"** concept ($R^2 > 0.97$), physically normalizing spatial wake convection delay and temporal phase differences.
- **Optimal Space–Time Coupling:** Pinpointed optimal parameters ($\Delta x / L = 0.5$, $\Delta \phi = 240^\circ$) where the posterior fin harvests wake vortex energy to achieve thrust exceeding that of an isolated fin.
