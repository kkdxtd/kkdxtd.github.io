**IAMReX** is an open-source, massively parallel adaptive framework designed for multiphase flows and fluid–particle interactions, developed within the AMReX ecosystem with C++/MPI/GPU acceleration.

**Contributions:**
- Code contributor to the solver (GitHub: [kkdxtd](https://github.com/kkdxtd))
- Participated in the algorithm implementation and integration of the multi-direct forcing Immersed Boundary Method (IBM) module for moving solid boundaries and particle–fluid coupling.
