## Overview

*(Reserved Showcase Slot / 预留项目展示区)*

This research focus explores the frontier intersection between **quantum computation and computational fluid dynamics (Quantum CFD)**, with a particular focus on the Lattice Boltzmann Method (LBM).

## Research Directions & Vision

- **Linear & Non-linear Quantum Formulations:** Investigating quantum algorithms for solving the discrete Boltzmann equation, mapping streaming (linear shift operators) and collision steps onto unitary quantum gates and quantum state preparations.
- **Quantum Linear System Algorithms (QLSA):** Analyzing quantum linear solvers (HHL, block encoding, and quantum singular value transformation) for sparse hydrodynamic matrices.
- **Potential Quantum Speedup:** Evaluating theoretical speedup limits (logarithmic complexity scaling in spatial grid resolution) versus state-preparation and measurement overheads in practical CFD problems.

- **Status:** Active literature tracking & theoretical formulation; reserved showcase for future graduate research and potential PhD exploration.
