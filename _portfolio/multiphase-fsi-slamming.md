## Overview

*(Reserved Showcase Slot / 预留项目展示区)*

Building upon my undergraduate background in Naval Architecture & Ocean Engineering (HEU) and seakeeping CFD, this project space is dedicated to high-fidelity numerical simulations of **violent free-surface slamming, water entry, and coupled Fluid–Structure Interaction (FSI)**.

## Core Simulation Capabilities

- **Multiphase Free-Surface Capturing:** Volume of Fluid (VOF) and Level-Set methods for highly non-linear wave breaking, spray, and air entrapment.
- **Extreme Slamming Pressures:** Resolving acoustic/incompressible pressure peaks during wedge water entry and bottom slamming on marine hulls.
- **Coupled FSI:** Coupling CFD flow fields with structural elastic-plastic response under hydrodynamic impact.
